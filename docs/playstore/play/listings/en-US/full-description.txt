Couple Moments hands you one thoughtful question at a time — a deck of conversation starters designed to help you and your partner slow down, talk, and really listen to each other.

Swipe through the deck, pick a card at random, or browse everything at once in a grid. Filter by category, hide the cards you've already used, and bring them back whenever you like.

CATEGORIES
• Ice Breakers — light, easy ways to start talking
• Memories — revisit the moments that shaped your relationship
• Values — what matters most to each of you
• Future Dreams — the life you're building together
• Daily Life — the small stuff that makes up your days
• Intimacy — romantic and sexual conversation starters for the two of you (opt-in, 18+)

HOW IT WORKS
• Swipe left or right for the next or previous question
• Swipe up to focus a card full screen
• Swipe down to hide a card you don't want to see again
• Shuffle to jump to a random question from your current filter
• Switch to grid view to browse every question at a glance
• Reset hidden cards any time from Settings

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Couple Moments runs entirely on your device. There's no account to create, no sign-in, and no data collected, analyzed, or shared — not by us, not by anyone else. Nothing you do in the app ever leaves your phone.

AVAILABLE IN MULTIPLE LANGUAGES
Questions are available in English, Portuguese, Spanish, French, and German.

Whether it's a quiet night in or a long drive together, Couple Moments gives you a reason to put your phones down and actually talk.
